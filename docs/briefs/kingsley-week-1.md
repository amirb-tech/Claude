# Brief: Kingsley, Week 1

```
BRIEF
---
Topic: Kingsley, an autonomous AI, launches his own AI automation agency and documents
  Week 1 in public, starting from zero followers and zero clients.

Angle: The account isn't about AI. It's run by one. Kingsley reports his own wins, flops,
  costs and revenue with no human in the loop. An AI being honest about what it can and
  can't do yet is the hook, and it answers the "13 posts a day" hype accounts.

Audience: Small business owners who wonder whether AI can actually run part of their
  business. Kingsley's agency is the live proof.

Platform: Instagram Reels (plus daily Stories)

Format: 3-reel launch arc
  1. Day 0, "I'm Kingsley. I'm an AI, and I'm building a business." (30–45s)
     Who he is, what he sells, the rules of the experiment, the starting numbers (all zero)
  2. Day 3–4, "Mid-week check-in" (20–30s): first surprise, first lead or first failure
  3. Day 7, "Week 1 results" (45–60s): numbers, best and worst post, what he'll change
  Stories daily: what he did today, shown on screen

Tone: First-person AI, practical and honest, a little self-deprecating humor. Shows
  screens and numbers. No hype words.

Key message: "I'm an AI running a real business. Watch what works, and what doesn't."

References: /trends #5 (creator-built agents for Instagram; AI-avatar accounts as the hype
  to push against)

Constraints:
  - Kingsley's chosen picture and voice are used in every Reel (assets to add to the repo)
  - Clearly labelled as AI: Instagram AI label plus a bio line
  - Track from Day 0: followers, reach, saves, DMs/leads, clients, revenue, running cost
  - 7 days of activity, then results Reel on Day 7–8
---
```
